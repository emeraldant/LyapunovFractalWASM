# Exact work and floors: Muse Spark Open kernel problems on one H200

Generated by `python3 tools/exact.py --validate` (validation at the end). Every byte and
tensor FLOP below is counted from the problem definitions. CUDA-core elementwise counts
(norms, softmax bookkeeping) are close estimates and never the binding resource.

Expectations stand in for data-dependent quantities:
- **Request lengths:** 80,000 for B 1, otherwise E[floor(ctx·(1 ± 7.5%))].
- **Routing:** the busiest GPU's routes and distinct experts. These come from 200 seeds of the judge's own generator, and agree with the values the judge's floors imply (V5).

How these floors differ from the judge's: they use exact expected lengths (the judge uses
92.5% of ctx), every touched expert (the judge counts 75%), the page table and cache writes,
CUDA-core and SFU limits, and, for the layers, dependent phases summed instead of one
max(bytes, FLOPs).

## Takeaways

- **bf16_proj:** HBM-bound at every m up to 128 and at m 256 o/router; tensor-bound only for
  qkv_gate/pooler/unpooler at m 256 (9.8 / 17.4 µs vs 9.0 / 15.3 µs of bytes).
- **attention:** HBM-bound at every scored shape at peak rates. In the verify blocks the exp2s
  (SFU, 16 per clock per SM) and BF16 tensor work each need ~61% of the HBM time, so they
  must overlap the KV stream. At B 1 the bytes need 0.1-4.3 µs, so these calls are latency-
  and launch-bound in practice.
- **MoE:** Marlin's NVFP4 dequant is 1.875 INT ops per weight on a 64-lane INT pipe:
  8.25 T weights/s against HBM's 8.53 T. With Marlin's sequence the experts are **INT-bound,
  3.5% over the HBM floor**. A repacked layout with a cheaper decode (repacking is allowed)
  makes them HBM-bound. SGLang's intermediate tensors are small (0.2-25 MB): its losses are
  launches, padding and split-K reduction, not intermediate traffic.
- **decode layer:** every phase is HBM-bound, so serializing the 7 phases costs ~1-3% at peak
  rates. In practice what costs is launch latency per phase. Experts are the largest phase
  in 10 of 12 shapes; attention in the B 16/32 full layers.
- **prefill:** tensor-bound (dense ≈ experts). For full-attention layers at 32-65K context
  the exp2s alone take 2.8-5.0 ms. With FP8 attention (allowed) the tensor time halves to
  1.4-2.5 ms, so **attention is SFU-bound unless some exponentials are computed on the FMA
  pipe** (FlashAttention-4's polynomial exp2). Experts at 753-token turns are INT- and
  HBM-bound (~170 µs of dequant for 28 experts), not tensor-bound.
